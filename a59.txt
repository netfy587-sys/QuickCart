<section id="rule">
  <div class="wrap">
    <p class="eyebrow">THE CORE IDEA</p>
    <h2 class="display head">You need three outfits, not one</h2>
    <p class="lede">Most first-time racers plan for the run and forget the two hours around it. Split race morning into three separate outfit problems and each one gets much easier to solve.</p>

    <div class="rule-grid">
      <div class="rule-card">
        <div class="num display">№1</div>
        <h3 class="display">The throwaway layer</h3>
        <p>An old hoodie or bin bag worn at the start corral, ditched right before the gun. Keeps you warm without warming up your actual race kit.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="rule-card">
        <div class="num display">№2</div>
        <h3 class="display">The race outfit</h3>
        <p>Only what you'll wear for the next few hours of moving. Lighter than feels sensible while standing still — you'll warm up fast once you're going.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="rule-card">
        <div class="num display">№3</div>
        <h3 class="display">The change bag</h3>
        <p>Dry clothes waiting at the finish. Wet, salt-stiff race kit gets uncomfortable fast the moment you stop moving and cool down.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section id="timeline" class="paper2" style="background:var(--paper2);">
  <div class="wrap">
    <p class="eyebrow">HOUR BY HOUR</p>
    <h2 class="display head">A typical race morning, layer by layer</h2>
    <p class="lede">Times shift depending on your race, but the sequence of what comes off and when stays roughly the same.</p>

    <div class="timeline">
      <div class="tl-item">
        <div class="time">−2h</div>
        <div><h4 class="display">Wake &amp; travel</h4><p>Wear the throwaway layer over your race outfit. It's easier to dress once at home than to change at a crowded venue.</p></div>
      </div>
      <div class="tl-item">
        <div class="time">−45m</div>
        <div><h4 class="display">Bag drop</h4><p>Hand off your change bag before you queue for the start corral — bag trucks usually close well before the gun.</p></div>
      </div>
      <div class="tl-item">
        <div class="time">−15m</div>
        <div><h4 class="display">Corral wait</h4><p>Standing still in the cold is the coldest part of your whole morning. Keep the throwaway layer on until the last possible minute.</p></div>
      </div>
      <div class="tl-item">
        <div class="time">0</div>
        <div><h4 class="display">Gun time</h4><p>Drop the throwaway layer to the side — most races collect and donate them. You're now in race kit only.</p></div>
      </div>
      <div class="tl-item">
        <div class="time">+2–4h</div>
        <div><h4 class="display">On the course</h4><p>Sun, wind, and effort all change how warm your kit feels versus how it felt standing at the start line.</p></div>
      </div>
      <div class="tl-item">
        <div class="time">Finish</div>
        <div><h4 class="display">The crash</h4><p>Body temperature drops fast once you stop. Dry layers from your change bag go on within minutes, not after the medal photos.</p></div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section>
  <div class="wrap">
    <p class="eyebrow">SPECIFICS</p>
    <h2 class="display head">Two details worth getting right</h2>
    <p class="lede">Small choices that separate a comfortable race from a distracting one.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="wrap">
    <div class="feat-row">
      <div class="media">
        <img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1502904550040-7534597429ae?w=900&auto=format&fit=crop&q=80" alt="A group of runners together on a stadium track before a race">
      </div>
      <div class="copy">
        <p class="eyebrow">BIB PLACEMENT</p>
        <h3 class="display">Pin it flat, pin it early</h3>
        <p>A bib pinned the night before saves fumbling in a crowded corral. Pin through all four corners so it doesn't flap, fold, or block the timing strip on the back.</p>
        <ul class="pin-list">
          <li>Pin over your race outfit, not the throwaway layer</li>
          <li>Keep it flat — a folded bib can miss timing mats</li>
          <li>Skip pinning it to a jacket you plan to remove</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="feat-row" style="margin-top:24px;">
      <div class="media" style="order:2;">
        <img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1632650312771-52c324fb4d83?w=900&auto=format&fit=crop&q=80" alt="An inflatable finish line arch at a race">
      </div>
      <div class="copy" style="order:1;">
        <p class="eyebrow">CARRYING ESSENTIALS</p>
        <h3 class="display">Pockets over pouches</h3>
        <p>A running belt or built-in pockets beat carrying anything by hand for hours. Keep gels, a phone, and cash for after — a bulky waist pouch tends to bounce and chafe by the halfway mark.</p>
        <ul class="pin-list">
          <li>Test any belt or pack on a training run first</li>
          <li>Keep pockets accessible without stopping</li>
          <li>Store finish-line ID or cash somewhere waterproof</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section id="mistakes">
  <div class="wrap">
    <p class="eyebrow">LEARNED THE HARD WAY</p>
    <h2 class="display head">Common race-day outfit mistakes</h2>
    <p class="lede">Nearly all of these come from testing something for the first time on race morning instead of in training.</p>

    <div class="mistake-row">
      <div class="mistake-col dont">
        <h4 class="display">Skip this</h4>
        <ul>
          <li>Wearing brand-new shoes for the first time on race day</li>
          <li>Dressing for how the start corral feels, not the whole race</li>
          <li>Cotton anything — it stays wet and heavy once it's soaked</li>
          <li>New socks straight out of the packet, unwashed</li>
          <li>Forgetting a hat or cap when the forecast says sun</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
      <div class="mistake-col do">
        <h4 class="display">Do this instead</h4>
        <ul>
          <li>Run at least one long training session in full race kit</li>
          <li>Dress about 10° warmer than the corral actually feels</li>
          <li>Pick synthetic or merino fabric, tested on a prior run</li>
          <li>Wear socks that have already survived a wash cycle</li>
          <li>Pack layers you can remove, not just ones you can add</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section id="checklist" style="background:var(--paper2);">
  <div class="wrap">
    <p class="eyebrow">PACK THIS THE NIGHT BEFORE</p>
    <h2 class="display head">The change-bag checklist</h2>
    <p class="lede">Everything waiting for you at the finish line, so the crash after the race is short and dry.</p>

    <div class="checklist">
      <div class="chk"><div class="box"></div><p>Dry top and bottoms<span>Something that isn't your sweat-soaked race kit</span></p></div>
